# Yamina — privacy policy

Yamina collects no data. It has no servers, no analytics and no accounts, and
it sends nothing anywhere.

- **What it reads:** on the sites you switch it on for, it reads the text on
  the page, in your browser, only to decide which paragraphs are mostly Hebrew
  or Arabic. That text never leaves the page.
- **What it stores:** nothing of its own. Which sites are switched on is kept
  by Chrome itself, as the site access you granted the extension; you can see
  and remove it at `chrome://extensions` → Yamina → Details → Site access.
- **Permissions:** none at install. Clicking the Yamina button on a site asks
  Chrome for access to that site only; clicking it again gives the access back.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/arikw/yamina/issues.
